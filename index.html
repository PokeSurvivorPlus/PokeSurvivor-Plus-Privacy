PokeSurvivor Plus Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

PokeSurvivor Plus does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Its developer receives nothing from the extension: there are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

Stored on your device

Your PokeSurvivor Plus settings, key and controller bindings, favourite Pokémon, personal records, run history and a cached copy of the tier list are saved in your browser's local storage on pokesurvivor.com. They never leave your computer unless you export them yourself with Settings → Extras → Backup. Removing the extension or clearing the site's data deletes them.

Shared with other players

In a co-op room, the extension sends some information to the other players in that room, over the game's own player-to-player connection:

  • Chat messages you type, and pings you place.
  • Game stats such as damage dealt, your Pokémon's upgrade level and pause votes.

This goes only to the players in your room, while you play. It does not pass through any server run by the developer, and the extension does not store it after the session.

Outside sites

The extension downloads two kinds of public files. No personal information is sent with these requests.

WhatFromWhen
Tier list (a CSV of rankings)survivortiers.pages.devOnly after you turn on Tiers, at most once a day
Pokémon portrait pictures for the HUDSpriteCollab on raw.githubusercontent.comDuring runs

Those sites receive the ordinary information any web request carries, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Runs on pokesurvivor.com: to add its features to the game. It does not run on any other site and does not read your browsing history.
  • survivortiers.pages.dev: to download the tier list file described above.
  • Tabs (own tab only): to keep the game tab from being discarded by Chrome's memory saver, and to close it when you choose Quit.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
